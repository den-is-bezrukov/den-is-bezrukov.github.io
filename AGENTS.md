# den-is-bezrukov.github.io

Denis Bezrukov's portfolio page: static HTML (no build), two pages. `index.html` is the hub: summary and a short preview of each project. `cases/index.html` holds the full texts, one `<article>` per project with the same id as its preview (`cases/#umo`), sections as `<h3>`; once a project's full text exists, its preview title and its entry in the project list at the top of the home page link there instead of to the preview. Both pages share `style.css` and the Typograf setup in `typograf-ru.js`. Deployed to GitHub Pages only on his word. It is his user site: the GitHub repository is `den-is-bezrukov/den-is-bezrukov.github.io`, the page lives at `den-is-bezrukov.github.io`, and the resume links to cases by their ids (`den-is-bezrukov.github.io/#umo`), so don't rename the ids. Texts are Russian; answer him in Russian.

- Each project preview is an `<article>` with a title, a grey line (client · role · period), two or three short paragraphs and a credits line. Write like Maxim Ilyakhov: past-tense verbs, facts and numbers, no self-praise, his honest share. Who did what comes from the Behance case credits; don't widen his role beyond them.
- Layout is deliberately default, like a Claude artifact or Notion page: Geist (the variable GeistVariable.woff2 from Vercel's Geist 1.8.2, kept in `fonts/` with its licence), one 680px column, spacing instead of dividers (base 16px, every size and gap a whole step of it in rem: headings H1 / H2 / H3 at 3 / 2 / 1.5rem set solid with a tracking of -3% (2 / 1.5 / 1.25rem on phones), each tag one size on both pages and bold in the text at 600, the intro under the name 1.5rem at 500 (1.25rem on phones), text and grey service lines 1rem on a 1.5 line; every margin, padding and gap from the spacing scale `--space-050`…`--space-800`, named by rem × 100. Blocks have no margins: each gap between two blocks is a space block in the page, `<div class="space space-150"></div>` (100, 150, 200, 300, 400, 500, 600), so every gap is set where it is (as laid out now: 150 between paragraphs and under a title, 300 before a section heading and between groups, 200 under H1, 100 under the intro and the summary); only projects stand 800 apart by the CSS (600 on phones), and list items 050), a white background (#fafafa, LoveFrom's, in edit mode only), black text (#000; the dark theme, black with text at 90% white #e6e6e6, is commented out for now), grey #999999, text at 65% in the dark theme.
- Non-breaking spaces are placed in the browser by Typograf (`typograf.min.js`, 7.8.0, kept next to the page; the script at the end of the page applies his `ru-typograph` rules, keeps hyphenated words and Latin names (CSS Design Awards) whole and never leaves the first word of a sentence or of a clause after a comma alone at a line end), so don't type `&nbsp;` into the copy yourself: the only ones in the files are those Denis sets by hand in edit mode.
- Images from client work: only what is already public on Behance; TXN Pro and UMO need his say first.
- Edit mode for the copy: `_edit/Править.command` (or `python3 _edit/server.py`) serves the site at localhost:8765 for viewing; with `?edit` in the address every heading and paragraph of that page is editable in place, and saves each change straight into that page's file. Lines break as on the site: Typograf runs in edit mode too and lays a block out again when you leave it, and the editor strips its non-breaking spaces and word spans before saving. There Enter (and Shift+Enter) sets a line break (`<br>`; nothing in rows of links and breadcrumbs), ⌘Enter adds an empty paragraph below the block wherever the caret is, ⇧⌘Enter cuts it at the caret into a new paragraph (in a list or a row of links it adds one below, as ⌘Enter), Backspace at a paragraph's start joins it to a plain paragraph above (an empty one is removed), ⌥Space a non-breaking space (saved as `&nbsp;`), ⌘K or pasting an address over selected words a link, and ⌘-click follows a link (the site's own pages open in edit mode too). The block with the caret is marked by a light grey (#f0f0f0) shape that follows its lines; a white toolbar in Geist Mono (kept in `_edit/`; every panel of the editor is set at 14px), capital labels in groups with a 0.5px line between them, sits right on top of it, on its left (held at the window's top once the block's start scrolls above it); with words selected it holds only link (as ⌘K). Otherwise: the block's style (h1, h2, lead, body, meta; h3 only on a page that has section headings; ⌥⌘1/2/3/0/8, lead has no shortcut), list, even, a switch for the line layout (on: the `balance` class, lines of nearly equal length; off: the site's default, pretty) and ↑ ↓ to move it (⌘⇧↑/↓; a project title moves the whole project and its entry in the project list). List is one block, a `<ul>` with an `<li>` per item: Enter in an item makes the next one (Shift+Enter breaks the line inside it), Enter in an empty last item leaves the list for a new paragraph, Backspace at an item's start joins it to the item above; List works as a switch (pressed on a list, it makes it Body again), and switching a block into List or out of it turns its lines into items and back. Every space shows at the column's right edge as a line across its middle, as long as the space is tall, and as its height in pixels under the pointer; a click on a space focuses it, and its toolbar, at the column's right edge, holds only its height in pixels (16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 80, 96 for the classes 100 … 600); spaces don't move, and Backspace or Delete removes one. A new paragraph (⌘Enter, ⇧⌘Enter) comes with a 150 space above it; joining two paragraphs or leaving an empty one removes the space between; ↑ ↓ on a text block pass over the spaces to the next text block and leave the spaces in place. Anchors belong to projects only: each project's anchor stands in grey capitals (#UMO) in the gap above it, at the column's right edge, and is typed into right there; Enter or leaving it puts the new one on, and links to it on both pages follow (`#umo` on its page, `cases/#umo` or `../#umo` from the other), Escape brings the old one back, an empty one takes it off along with the project's line in the project list. The shortcuts panel is out for now. ⌘Z/⇧⌘Z step through saves. Saves go to the server one at a time, each checked against what the file should hold; every write keeps the version it replaces in `_edit/history/` (git-ignored, the last 200 per page), and undo refuses once the file was changed from outside the editor. `_edit/` starts with an underscore, so GitHub Pages doesn't publish it.
