# den-is-bezrukov.github.io

Denis Bezrukov's portfolio page: static HTML (no build), two pages. `index.html` is the hub: summary and a short preview of each project. `cases/index.html` holds the full texts, one `<article>` per project with the same id as its preview (`cases/#umo`), sections as `<h3>`; once a project's full text exists, its preview title and its entry in the project list at the top of the home page link there instead of to the preview. Both pages share `style.css` and the Typograf setup in `typograf-ru.js`. Deployed to GitHub Pages only on his word. It is his user site: the GitHub repository is `den-is-bezrukov/den-is-bezrukov.github.io`, the page lives at `den-is-bezrukov.github.io`, and the resume links to cases by their ids (`den-is-bezrukov.github.io/#umo`), so don't rename the ids. Texts are Russian; answer him in Russian.

- Each project preview is an `<article>` with a title, a grey line (client · role · period), two or three short paragraphs and a credits line. Write like Maxim Ilyakhov: past-tense verbs, facts and numbers, no self-praise, his honest share. Who did what comes from the Behance case credits; don't widen his role beyond them.
- Layout is deliberately default, like a Claude artifact or Notion page: Inter (Google Fonts, variable with the optical size axis, so big headings get Inter Display's shapes by themselves), one 680px column, spacing instead of dividers (base 16px, every size and gap a whole step of it in rem: headings 3 / 2rem set solid (2 / 1.5rem on phones) and bold in the text at 600, the intro under the name 1.25rem, text and grey service lines 1rem, the grey at 500, a full line between paragraphs; gaps `--gap` 8rem and `--gap-s` 4rem, 6 and 3rem on phones), black and white backgrounds with text at 90% black or white (#1a1a1a / #e6e6e6), grey #808080 on white, text at 65% in the dark theme.
- Non-breaking spaces are placed in the browser by Typograf (`typograf.min.js`, 7.8.0, kept next to the page; the script at the end of the page applies his `ru-typograph` rules, keeps hyphenated words whole and never leaves the first word of a sentence or of a clause after a comma alone at a line end), so don't type `&nbsp;` into the copy.
- Images from client work: only what is already public on Behance; TXN Pro and UMO need his say first.
- Edit mode for the copy: `_edit/Править.command` (or `python3 _edit/server.py`) serves the site at localhost:8765 for viewing; with `?edit` in the address every heading and paragraph of that page is editable in place, and saves each change straight into that page's file. `_edit/` starts with an underscore, so GitHub Pages doesn't publish it.
