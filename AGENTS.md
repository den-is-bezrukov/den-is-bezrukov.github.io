# den-is-bezrukov.github.io

Denis Bezrukov's portfolio page: static HTML (no build), two pages. `index.html` is the hub: summary and a short preview of each project. `cases/index.html` holds the full texts, one `<article>` per project with the same id as its preview (`cases/#umo`), sections as `<h3>`; while a project's full text is shown there (not `hidden`), its preview title and its entry in the project list at the top of the home page link there instead of to the preview; the edit-mode server sets these links itself on every save and at start, so they are never set by hand, and a full text still being written stays hidden in cases. Both pages share `style.css` and the Typograf setup in `typograf-ru.js`. Deployed to GitHub Pages only on his word. It is his user site: the GitHub repository is `den-is-bezrukov/den-is-bezrukov.github.io`, the page lives at `den-is-bezrukov.github.io`, and the resume links to cases by their ids (`den-is-bezrukov.github.io/#umo`), so don't rename the ids. Texts are Russian; answer him in Russian.

- Each project preview is an `<article>` with a title, a grey line (client · role · period), two or three short paragraphs and a credits line. Write like Maxim Ilyakhov: past-tense verbs, facts and numbers, no self-praise, his honest share. Who did what comes from the Behance case credits; don't widen his role beyond them.
- Layout is deliberately default, like a Claude artifact or Notion page: Geist (the variable GeistVariable.woff2 from Vercel's Geist 1.8.2, kept in `fonts/` with its licence), one 680px column, spacing instead of dividers (base 16px, every size and gap a whole step of it in rem: headings H1 / H2 / H3 at 3 / 2 / 1.5rem set solid, H1 alone with a tracking of -3% (2 / 1.5 / 1.25rem on phones), each tag one size on both pages and bold in the text at 500, the intro under the name 1.5rem at 500 (1.25rem on phones), text and grey service lines 1rem on a 1.5 line; every margin, padding and gap from the spacing scale `--space-050`…`--space-1000`, named by rem × 100. Blocks have no margins: each gap between two blocks is a space block in the page, `<div class="space space-150"></div>` (000, 050, 100, 150, 200, 300, 400, 500, 600), so every gap is set where it is (as laid out now: 150 between paragraphs and under a title, 300 before a section heading and between groups, 200 under H1, 100 under the intro and the summary); only projects stand apart by the CSS, one gap for the whole page: 800 unless `<main>` has gap-500, gap-600, gap-900 or gap-1000 (80, 96, 144 or 160px), a quarter less on phones, the home page's footer as far below the last project; and list items 050), a background of #fafafa (LoveFrom's), black text (#000; the dark theme, black with text at 90% white #e6e6e6, is commented out for now), grey #999999, text at 65% in the dark theme.
- Non-breaking spaces are placed in the browser by Typograf (`typograf.min.js`, 7.8.0, kept next to the page; the script at the end of the page applies his `ru-typograph` rules, keeps hyphenated words and Latin names (CSS Design Awards) whole and never leaves the first word of a sentence or of a clause after a comma alone at a line end), so don't type `&nbsp;` into the copy yourself: the only ones in the files are those Denis sets by hand in edit mode.
- The home page ends on a footer, a grey link «Все проекты» to `cases/`, 800 below the last project (600 on phones), left out of print.
- `denis-bezrukov.pdf` is the home page printed by the site's print styles (A4, margins 16mm, base 13px with every size and gap following it, every space at half its height, projects 300 apart (400 after the summary), no project list and no link to the PDF itself, rows of links on one line, a title never last on a page; 3 pages now), linked as PDF in the contacts. `python3 _edit/pdf.py` makes it again with the Chrome on this Mac; run it after editing the home page, before the site goes out.
- Images from client work: only what is already public on Behance; TXN Pro and UMO need his say first.
- Edit mode for the copy: `_edit/Править.command` (or `python3 _edit/server.py`) serves the site at localhost:8765 for viewing; with `?edit` in the address every heading and paragraph of that page is editable in place, and saves each change straight into that page's file. Lines break as on the site: Typograf runs in edit mode too and lays a block out again when you leave it, and the editor strips its non-breaking spaces and word spans before saving. There Enter (and Shift+Enter) sets a line break (`<br>`; nothing in breadcrumbs; in a row of links Enter breaks the line between items (or cuts the item at the caret onto the next line) and Backspace right after the break takes it off, a typed · cuts the item at the caret into two, the dot drawn by the row, spaces around it dropped, and Backspace right after a dot joins the two items back), ⌘Enter adds an empty paragraph below the block wherever the caret is, ⇧⌘Enter cuts it at the caret into a new paragraph (in a list or a row of links it adds one below, as ⌘Enter), Backspace at a paragraph's start joins it to a plain paragraph above (an empty one is removed), ⌥Space a non-breaking space (saved as `&nbsp;`), ⌘K or pasting an address over selected words a link (a bare site gets https://, an e-mail mailto:, a phone number tel: with digits only; in the link field × takes a link off, / offers the other pages, and a page picked keeps the field open on it, the page itself first and its projects under it; # this page's projects; hidden projects are offered too, and an address typed out by hand from / goes in as the site writes it; ↑ ↓ and Enter pick one, written as the site writes its links: cases/#umo, ../), and ⌘-click follows a link (the site's own pages open in edit mode too). The block with the caret is marked by a light grey (#f0f0f0) shape that follows its lines; a white toolbar in Geist Mono (kept in `_edit/`; every panel of the editor is set at 14px), capital labels in groups with a 0.5px line between them, sits right on top of it, on its left (held at the window's top once the block's start scrolls above it); with words selected it stands over the first of them and holds only bold (as ⌘B; on bold words it takes the bold off) and link (as ⌘K). Otherwise: the block's style (h1, h2, lead, body, meta; h3 only on a page that has section headings; ⌥⌘1/2/3/0/8, lead has no shortcut), list, even, a switch for the line layout (on: the `balance` class, lines of nearly equal length; off: the site's default, pretty) and ↑ ↓ to move it (⌘⇧↑/↓; a project title moves the whole project and its entry in the project list). List is one block, a `<ul>` with an `<li>` per item: Enter in an item makes the next one (Shift+Enter breaks the line inside it), Enter in an empty last item leaves the list for a new paragraph, Backspace at an item's start joins it to the item above; List works as a switch (pressed on a list, it makes it Body again), and switching a block into List or out of it turns its lines into items and back. Every space shows in the middle of the column as a 24px line across it, and, under the pointer or when picked, as its edges drawn across the column with its height in pixels in grey in the middle, its toolbar, when picked, over its middle in place of the height; a click on a space focuses it, and its toolbar, centred over it, holds only its height in pixels (0, 8, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 80, 96 for the classes 000, 050, 100 … 600; a 0 space is found by a 4px reach up and down) and, while it is set off the usual gap for its place, ↩ to bring it back (32 under H1, 48 before an H3 and closing a group, 16 under the intro and the summary, 24 elsewhere); spaces don't move and can't be removed on their own: they come and go with their paragraphs. A new paragraph (⌘Enter, ⇧⌘Enter) comes with a 150 space above it; joining two paragraphs or leaving an empty one removes the space between; ↑ ↓ on a text block pass over the spaces to the next text block and leave the spaces in place. Anchors belong to projects only: each project's anchor stands in grey capitals (#UMO, 16px) at the bottom of the gap above it, 16px over the project, at the column's left edge, and is typed into right there; Enter or leaving it puts the new one on, on both pages at once (a project goes by one anchor: its preview on the home page and its full text in cases), and every link to them follows (`#umo`, `cases/#umo`, `../#umo`); an anchor taken by something else on either page is refused; Escape, or leaving it empty, brings the old one back (a project never goes without an anchor). Likewise a row of links or the breadcrumbs wiped out whole get their words back on leaving: the page's frame never goes empty in the editor; taking one away for good is done by hand. Under the pointer a toolbar 8px above the anchor holds + (a new project from the template right here, this one going down, its anchor selected to type over), ↑ ↓ (the whole project moves, with its line in the project list, and the other page's projects follow the same order); while the anchor is typed into, at the right end of its line: Hide (Show on a hidden one) takes the project off the site with its line in the project list (`hidden` on the article and the line; the editor still shows the project, faint, but not its line), ╳ takes it off this page (the other page keeps its own). A link to a project (a line of the project list, or a link in the text to #umo, cases/#umo, ../#umo) follows the project's title as long as it reads the same: a title changed changes the links that matched it, a link typed otherwise keeps its words, and while the caret is in such a link, set apart from the title or written with another address, the toolbar holds ↩ to bring back both the title and the usual address (on the home page cases/#umo while the project's full text is shown in cases, #umo otherwise). The gap above a project (all but the first one under the cases page's header) shows its edges and its height under the pointer; a click on the gap itself picks it, with 80, 96, 128, 144, 160 in its middle and, while it is set off the usual 128, ↩ to bring it back, and the choice sets the gap between all the projects of the page at once. The shortcuts panel is out for now. ⌘Z/⇧⌘Z step through saves. Saves go to the server one at a time, each checked against what the file should hold; every write keeps the version it replaces in `_edit/history/` (git-ignored, the last 200 per page), and undo refuses once the file was changed from outside the editor. `_edit/` starts with an underscore, so GitHub Pages doesn't publish it. The same edit mode runs in GitHub Codespaces (`.devcontainer/`): the server starts when the codespace opens and its port opens in the browser (add `?edit`); edits there go to the site after a commit and a push from the codespace.
